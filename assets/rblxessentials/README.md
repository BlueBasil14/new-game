# RBLX Essentials Sound System 3.2.2

Downloaded from the creator: https://rblx-essentials.itch.io/sound-system
Direct official download: https://rblxessentials.com/download/free/SoundSystem
Documentation: https://rblxessentials.com/ai/SoundSystem.md

The five service models preserve the supplied Kernel, scripts, GUI, 15 music tracks,
and 42 sound effects. They are mapped by default.project.json and included by
tools/build_place.py. No demo pads are installed. The music HUD is inset below the
leaderboard and away from the phone camera cutout. Other pack content is unchanged.

Game sound effects remain in FeedbackController; this adds the pack's independent
music player and volume controls. Existing game effects are not remapped to pack names.
