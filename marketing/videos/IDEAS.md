# Turf Loop shorts: 25 pixel-art ideas

_Lead session, 2026-11-19. Brainstorm for the owner's request: short videos in the 8/16-bit, beat-synced chiptune "satisfying explainer" style, about Turf Loop, with nostalgia hooks (the territory .io game everyone played at school). Research behind it: `docs/SHORT_FORM_RESEARCH.md`._

## Goal and constraints (one line)
25 vertical 15–30 s videos that stop the scroll in 2 s with a Turf Loop payoff, look like crisp pixel art, land every action on a beat, and end on "Turf Loop, coming to Roblox", using only original music and art.

**Rules the ideas follow**
- **Payoff first.** A loop closing and the colour flood lands inside the first 2 s, because outcome-first hooks did best in the research.
- **Text on screen from frame 1.** About 40% of viewers watch with the sound off.
- **On the beat.** Runners move one cell per 16th note, so every step, claim, coin and cut lands on the grid of the original chiptune track.
- **Nostalgia without naming the other game.** Say "that game from computer class" rather than the trademark. It's safer, and it lets everyone fill in their own memory (Paper.io, splix, the Snake-likes).
- **Kid-safe.** No fake prizes, no "free Robux", and no bought views.

**Recommendation:** lead with **#1 Computer Lab**. It is the most distinctive hook, the one nobody else can copy, and it carries the whole message (nostalgia, now on Roblox, play with friends) in 25 s. First step: build the shared pixel renderer and chiptune generator, render #1–#5, have them graded, then work through the rest.

## Production notes
- Engine: `tools/video/engine.py` (Pillow + ffmpeg, no paid tools). It draws at 270×480 and scales ×4 with nearest-neighbour to 1080×1920, at 30 fps. Music and sound effects are synthesised in code from the same beat grid, so they are original and licence-free.
- Each video's script is in `tools/video/videos.py`. Output goes to `marketing/videos/out/` (mp4s are not committed), contact sheets to `marketing/videos/sheets/`, and grades to `marketing/videos/GRADES.md`.
- Grading: each video is scored /10 by independent reviewer subagents from its contact sheet, timeline and audio plan. Anything under 7 is reworked and graded again.
