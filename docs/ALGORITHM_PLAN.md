## Week-two plan (decide before launch)
Ship on day 8–10 after launch, announced in-game from day 5 with a countdown on the HUD:
1. **Streak beyond day 7**: days 8–28 keep paying, with a bigger reward on days 14, 21 and 28 (an exclusive skin on day 28). Today the streak is a 7-day table that loops; the second loop repeats the same currency and the day-7 skin is granted only once, so there is nothing new to come back for after week one.
2. **Weekly event "Crown Rush"**: one weekend where the King earns double and a limited trail is the prize for holding the crown for a total of 5 minutes.
3. **Two new skins and one new trail** in the Featured tab, so returning players see something new.
4. **A code** posted in the group on update day.

None of this is built. Item 1 is small and server-side (RewardService and the catalogue); items 2–4 are content.

## Order of work
1. T-RET-01 friend bonus and invite (in progress).
2. Measure time to first capture and first-minute bounce risks in a playtest.
3. Streak days 8–28.
4. Finish the 8 missing sounds, then the owner records the gameplay video.
5. Owner: passes, products, badges, group; publish; then ads and the nomination form.
