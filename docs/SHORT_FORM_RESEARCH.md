# Short-form content plan for Turf Loop: research

_Written 2026-11-19 by the lead from web research (sources at the bottom). Most published numbers on Roblox marketing come from agencies that sell marketing (BLOXG, MetaClip and others), so treat their figures as rough guides. **Time estimates marked (est.) are mine, not from a source.**_

## The short version
1. **The cheapest and strongest option is organic TikTok plus YouTube Shorts, made from our own gameplay.** It costs $0 and takes about 30–60 min per clip once recording is set up. The sources agree it beats paid options for a new Roblox game.
2. **Our genre already suits the format.** Paper.io-style "loop closes, area floods with colour" footage is the kind of payoff the hook research asks for in the first 2 seconds. Paper.io 2 was one of the top three hypercasual downloads of 2025 (see `docs/research.md`).
3. **Hype that arrives before the game can hold players is wasted.** One analysis says a TikTok spike lasts 24–72 h, and players who bounce on day 1 hurt Roblox ranking for weeks. So the big push should land when the week-two plan in `ALGORITHM_PLAN.md` is ready, not before.
4. **Recommended budget: $0–150 before launch, $100–500 in launch week.** Spend it on 3–5 small Roblox creators and a small Roblox Ads test, not on TikTok ads.

## What is working right now (late 2026)
| Trend | What it is | Fit for Turf Loop |
|---|---|---|
| **Payoff in the first 2 s** | Show the result first: the big capture or the kill, then explain. Opus Pro's study of about 35k clips found "outcome showcase" hooks did best. | **Very high.** Open on a huge loop closing and the map flooding with colour. |
| **On-screen text in the first frame** | A lot of viewers watch with sound off (one source says about 40%). | High. Every clip gets a one-line caption. |
| **"POV: you join a Roblox server and…"** | First-person framing. Blogs report it beats plain gameplay clips. | High. "POV: you cut the King's trail with 1 second left" |
| **15–20 s length** | Recommended as the best length for reach. | High. A loop takes 5–20 s. |
| **Brainrot / meme edits** | Heavily edited clips with trending audio and absurd captions. The biggest Roblox trend of early 2026 (Steal a Brainrot). | Medium. Fine for some clips, but don't build the brand on it, because it dates fast. |
| **Satisfying timelapses** | Watching a map fill up over time. Territorial.io clips do well on TikTok. | **Very high.** A top-down timelapse of one runner taking 0% → 100% of the map. |
| **Rage / fail / clutch moments** | Getting cut 1 cell from home, head-on kills both players. | High. The game produces these on its own. |
| **Devlog / building in public** | "I'm making a Roblox game, day 12." Builds a following of people who care about the game. Clips about the gameplay itself find new players. | Medium. Good for TikTok, and it suits a solo owner. Mix it with gameplay clips. |
| **Making 3 hooks per clip** | Post the same clip with 3 different openings and keep whatever retains best. | Use it on every clip that performs well. |

**Posting rate the sources agree on:** 3–5 clips a week, every week. Gaps hurt more than any single clip helps. Raise the rate in the 2–3 weeks before launch.

## 15 clip ideas made for Turf Loop
1. **"0% to 100%"**: a top-down timelapse of one runner swallowing the whole map, ending on the 5,000-coin banner.
2. **"Biggest loop ever"**: a slow-motion close with a counter rolling up the cells captured.
3. **"POV: you cut the King"**: crown drops, double reward.
4. **"1 cell from home"**: getting cut right at the edge (a fail).
5. **"Head-on"**: both runners eliminated in the same tick.
6. **"Stealing their whole base"**: a loop that encloses an enemy's turf.
7. **"Rate my trail"**: a cosmetic showcase with each skin in action.
8. **"Bots vs me"**: a 16-runner chaos montage.
9. **Devlog: "I made Paper.io in Roblox"**: the hook every viewer gets instantly.
10. **Devlog: "Day X of making my Roblox game"**: a series.
11. **"Which colour wins?"**: a bot-only match with a poll in the comments.
12. **"Rebirth reveal"**: before/after power.
13. **Countdown clips**: "X days until Turf Loop", each with a new 3-second highlight.
14. **"Can you beat my score?"**: a challenge clip ending on the Notify-Me link.
15. **The 3-hook test**: the best clip re-cut with 3 different openings.

## Suggested schedule (not dated, since there is no launch date yet)
| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Now to T−4 weeks** | Set up OBS and an editor (A). Build the top-down camera (B). Make TikTok, YouTube and Discord accounts under the game's name. Post 3 clips a week (ideas 1, 2, 9, 10) to find which formats work. |
| **T−3 weeks** | Create the Roblox Event with Notify Me (K) and link it everywhere. Contact 10–15 small creators and aim for 3–5 deals at launch (E). Raise to 5 clips a week. |
| **T−1 week** | Countdown clips (13). Upload the game-page video (J). Send creators early access and a code. |
| **Launch day** | Post on every platform at the same time, in US after-school hours. Creator videos go live. Give a code to anyone who followed via the event. |
| **Launch + 1–2 weeks** | Boost only the clip that is already winning (I, $10–20). Small Roblox Ads test (H) once day-1 retention is healthy. Week-two update clips. |

**Minimum budget path:** $0. Options A, B, C, J, K and L only.
**Recommended path:** about $250–500 in total: 3–5 small creators (about $50–100 each), $20–40 to boost a winning clip, and $35–100 of Roblox Ads after launch.

## Risks to watch
- **Spiking too early.** A viral clip with no game to play, or with a weak week two, wastes the moment and can drag down ranking.
- **Clones.** A Roblox hit that goes viral often gets copied within days. Keep the name and look consistent in every clip so the original is easy to find.
- **Kid audience rules.** Paid creator posts must be clearly marked as ads. Don't run paid TikTok targeting at under-13s.
- **The game-page video** must be real gameplay with no voice-over, no lyrics and no cinematic trailer. Those rules come from the video the owner supplied and are unverified against Roblox's own docs.
- **Bought followers or views.** Don't. They hurt the algorithm's sense of who the audience is.
